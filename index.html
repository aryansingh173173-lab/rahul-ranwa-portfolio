Reel 03 · About

I make stories that move.

Hi, I'm Rahul. I'm a filmmaker and visual artist, and that's how I make sense of the world. Martin Scorsese said the most personal is the most creative, and that's how I'll sum this up.

Music is where most of my days begin and end; you'll usually find me with my headphones on, and often with a camera. I'm drawn to people and to moments that slip by unnoticed: the pauses, the glances, the things left unsaid. I love drama, colours, paintings, history, literature and cultures.

I've always wanted to do everything, but one life felt too small. Films changed that. They let me live many lives at once.

On the page

Scripts built for structure and clarity, then mood boards for tone, organised into decks so the whole crew sees the same film.

On the floor

Hand-drawn storyboards for every shot. Lighting a scene is my favourite part of cinematography.

With actors

Long casting and rehearsals, until actors know their characters better than I do. Heavy pre-production buys the freedom to experiment on set.

In the edit

Cut for story and pace, graded for a cinematic finish.
